Inverse-transform sampling: y = -(1/lambda) ln(u),  lambda = 1.0
N = 5000 samples (seed = 1)

sample mean   = 0.99976   (exact 1/lambda = 1.00000)
sample stddev = 1.02197   (exact 1/lambda = 1.00000)
For the exponential distribution the mean equals the standard deviation,
a clean one-number check that the transformation worked.
